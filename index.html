2026 intake cut-offs, as published by MOE. Indicative only. Data sources

Cut-off points. For the 2026 Secondary 1 intake (PSLE 2025, posted December 2025), from MOE SchoolFinder as reproduced by Ancourage Academy (6 Sep 2026) and Arche Academy (7 Oct 2026). Each range runs from the best score admitted to the cut-off, the score of the last student posted. A star (30*) means places were left. Letters such as (D) and (M) are Higher Chinese grades at Special Assistance Plan schools. 2027 intake figures come out after the 2026 PSLE results.

How fit is worked out. A score maps to the Posting Groups it can use (4 to 20: PG3, 21 to 22: PG2 or PG3, 23 to 24: PG2, 25: PG1 or PG2, 26 to 30: PG1 with AL7 or better in English and Maths). The school's cut-off in the most favourable group is compared with the score. Comfortable is 2 or more points inside, borderline is 0 to 1, stretch is 1 to 2 above. IP routes are compared too.

Roads, MRT/LRT stations and landmarks on the map are drawn from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), simplified for this page. Station and road names follow OpenStreetMap and may lag recent changes.

DSA-Sec talent areas are from KiasuParents' 2026 listing, grouped into categories here. Addresses, MRT, CCAs, mother tongues and programmes are from the MOE School Directory (as at March 2021), so a school that moved or merged since may be out of date. Map points were located from postal codes with OneMap.

Eight schools do not post through S1 Posting (for example School of the Arts, Singapore Sports School, NUS High) and show no cut-off. Confirm on MOE SchoolFinder before ranking choices. This page is a planning aid and is not affiliated with MOE.
